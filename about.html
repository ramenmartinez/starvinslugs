About us

A student-run snack stand that brings cheap food and drinks to where people already are.

Why we started

Getting a snack between classes shouldn't be a trek. The markets sit at the edges of campus, and the closer options are coffee shops and vending machines, where one granola bar can run $4. On hot or rainy days a lot of people just skip eating.

The real issue is access. There's almost nowhere in the middle of campus to grab food, and over a long day that wears on your energy, your mood, and your time.

So we bring the food to you

We set up in busy spots with bánh mì and boba at prices that make sense for students. Every part of it, from buying supplies to serving the line, is run by our team.

Our mission

Make snacks and drinks easy to get for students, meet them where they are, and build community through food.
